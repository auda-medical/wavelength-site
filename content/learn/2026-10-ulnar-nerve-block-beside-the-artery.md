Lacerations and fractures on the ulnar side of the hand, including boxer's fractures of the fifth metacarpal, often need analgesia for repair or manipulation. A forearm ulnar nerve block numbs the little finger, the ulnar half of the ring finger and the ulnar side of the palm. The ulnar artery is your landmark.

![Wavelength Pearl: ulnar nerve block. Find the ulnar artery first, then the ulnar nerve beside it, and surround the nerve with local anaesthetic.](/learn/images/ulnar-block-pearl.webp)

*Scan images in the graphic are illustrations, not patient scans.*

## Before you block

1. Record motor and sensory function of the hand, including two-point discrimination, before the block.
2. Record consent and calculate the dose. Three to five millilitres is usually enough.

## The scan

1. Rest the forearm palm up.
2. Place the linear probe transversely on the ulnar side of the forearm, just above the wrist.
3. Find the ulnar artery with colour Doppler. The nerve lies on its ulnar side, under flexor carpi ulnaris.
4. Slide the probe up the forearm. The nerve moves away from the artery, which gives a safer target.
5. Insert the needle in plane from the ulnar side, so it meets the nerve before the artery.
6. Inject small amounts and watch the fluid surround the nerve.

## Reading the view

- **Ulnar artery.** Round, black and pulsating, with colour on Doppler.
- **Ulnar nerve.** A small honeycomb oval on the ulnar side of the artery.
- **Flexor carpi ulnaris.** The muscle and tendon lying over the nerve.
- **Correct spread.** Fluid surrounding the nerve, away from the artery.

> **The aha** In the distal forearm the nerve hugs the artery. A few centimetres higher they part, and the block gets easier.

> **Pitfall** The dorsal cutaneous branch leaves the ulnar nerve about 5 cm above the wrist and supplies the back of the ulnar hand. For a wound on the back of the hand, block higher in the forearm or add a field block. Use Doppler so you do not mistake a vein for the nerve.

?? On which side of the ulnar artery does the ulnar nerve lie in the distal forearm?
+ The ulnar side, under flexor carpi ulnaris
- The radial side, under brachioradialis
- Inside the artery wall
- Deep to the radius
: The nerve runs beside the artery on its ulnar side, covered by flexor carpi ulnaris.

?? Why does sliding the probe up the forearm help?
+ The nerve moves away from the artery, giving a safer target
- The nerve becomes larger than the humerus
- The artery disappears
- The nerve moves to the back of the forearm
: Higher in the forearm, the nerve and artery separate, which reduces the risk of a vascular puncture.

?? A patient has a laceration on the back of the hand over the fifth metacarpal. Why might a distal ulnar block fail?
+ The dorsal cutaneous branch leaves about 5 cm above the wrist
- The ulnar nerve does not supply the hand
- The median nerve supplies the back of the little finger
- The radial nerve supplies the fifth metacarpal
: The dorsal cutaneous branch arises above the usual distal injection point. Block higher in the forearm or add a field block.

?? Which tool helps you avoid confusing the nerve with a small vessel?
+ Colour Doppler
- A peripheral nerve stimulator alone
- Pressing harder with the probe
- A curvilinear probe
: Colour Doppler shows flow in vessels. The nerve shows no flow.

?? Which area does an ulnar nerve block numb?
+ The little finger, the ulnar half of the ring finger and the ulnar side of the palm
- The thumb and index finger
- The radial side of the back of the hand
- The whole forearm
: The ulnar nerve supplies the little finger, half of the ring finger and the ulnar side of the hand.
