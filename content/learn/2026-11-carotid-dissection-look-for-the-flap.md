Cervical artery dissection is a tear in the arterial wall. Blood tracks into the wall, narrows the lumen and seeds clots that travel to the brain. It causes up to a fifth of strokes in people under 45, often after minor neck trauma or sudden neck movement. The clues are one-sided neck pain or headache, a partial Horner syndrome (a small pupil and a drooping lid), pulsatile tinnitus, and a transient or persisting neurological deficit.

## The scan

1. Use a high-frequency linear probe with the head turned slightly away.
2. Start low on the common carotid artery in transverse and follow it up to the bifurcation.
3. Follow the internal carotid artery as far up towards the jaw as it can be seen, in transverse and long axis.
4. Add colour and spectral Doppler in the common and internal carotid arteries, and compare with the other side.
5. If you can, look at the vertebral artery between the transverse processes as well.

## Reading the view

- **Normal:** one lumen with smooth, parallel walls and colour filling the whole lumen.
- **Intimal flap:** a thin bright line inside the lumen that moves with each pulse, with flow on both sides: a true and a false lumen.
- **Mural haematoma:** a crescent of dark, eccentric wall thickening that narrows the lumen and tapers over a length of artery, often without a free flap.
- **Indirect signs:** a high-resistance waveform with little or no diastolic flow, or very low flow, in the internal carotid, which suggests narrowing or occlusion higher up.

> **The aha** If you see a flap, think dissection.

> **Pitfall** A normal scan does not exclude dissection. Most internal carotid dissections begin 2 to 3 cm above the bifurcation and can extend to the skull base, out of reach. Reverberation artefact can mimic a flap: a true flap stays inside the lumen in both planes and has colour flow on both sides. Plaque in an older patient is not a flap.

## Action

Suspected dissection with neurology goes on the stroke pathway at once: the scan must not delay CT angiography, the stroke team or thrombolysis and thrombectomy decisions. Without neurology, arrange urgent CT or MR angiography. Antiplatelet or anticoagulant treatment is decided by the stroke team.

?? Which presentation should make you think of carotid dissection?
+ One-sided neck pain with a small pupil and a drooping eyelid on the same side
- Bilateral calf swelling
- A painless lump in the thyroid
- Pain on swallowing with a fever
: A partial Horner syndrome with neck pain or headache is a classic pattern of internal carotid dissection.

?? What does a true intimal flap look like?
+ A thin bright line inside the lumen, moving with the pulse, with flow on both sides
- A bright shadowing mass on the back wall
- A black space outside the artery
- Smooth parallel walls with one lumen
: A flap divides the artery into a true and a false lumen. Check it in two planes and with colour.

?? Why does a normal carotid scan not exclude dissection?
+ Most internal carotid dissections start above the reach of the probe
- Ultrasound cannot show arteries
- Dissections only occur in the vertebral arteries
- Colour Doppler is not used in the neck
: The usual site is 2 to 3 cm above the bifurcation, extending towards the skull base. CT or MR angiography is required.

?? The internal carotid shows a high-resistance waveform with almost no diastolic flow. What does it suggest?
+ A narrowing or occlusion further up the artery
- A normal internal carotid
- A pseudoaneurysm at the bifurcation
- Reverberation artefact
: The internal carotid normally has continuous diastolic flow because it supplies the brain. Loss of diastolic flow suggests obstruction downstream.

?? A 38-year-old has sudden right arm weakness and left neck pain. You see a possible flap. What next?
+ Activate the stroke pathway and obtain CT angiography without delay
- Repeat the ultrasound in 24 hours
- Discharge with analgesia, because the weakness is resolving
- Massage the neck to relieve the pain
: Neurology plus a suspected dissection is a stroke emergency. The scan supports suspicion; it does not replace the pathway.
