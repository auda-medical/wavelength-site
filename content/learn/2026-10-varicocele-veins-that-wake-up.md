A varicocele is a cluster of dilated veins around the spermatic cord. It is common, usually painless or a dull ache, and usually on the left, because the left testicular vein drains at a right angle into the left renal vein. Most are not urgent. Ultrasound confirms the diagnosis and, as importantly, checks the testis and the side.

![Wavelength Pearl: varicocele. Small cord veins at rest, dilated serpiginous veins, and veins enlarging with flow during Valsalva.](/learn/images/varicocele-pearl.webp)

*Scan images in the graphic are illustrations, not patient scans.*

## The scan

1. Explain the scan, offer a chaperone and record the offer.
2. With the patient lying flat, use a high-frequency linear probe above the testis, along the spermatic cord.
3. Measure the widest vein at rest, and add colour Doppler with a low scale.
4. Ask the patient to bear down (Valsalva), and watch the veins and the colour. Repeat standing if you can.
5. Scan both sides, and both testes.

## Reading the view

- **Normal:** a few small veins, usually under about 2 to 3 mm, with little change on straining.
- **Varicocele:** several tortuous veins over about 3 mm, which enlarge and fill with reversed flow on Valsalva or standing.
- **Testes:** compare their size. A smaller testis on the varicocele side matters for fertility referral.

> **The aha** Make it strain, then make the call.

> **Pitfall** A single still image misleads, and a varicocele can look small lying down. Add Valsalva, standing and colour before you decide.

## Action

Most varicoceles are referred routinely to urology or the GP, especially with pain, a smaller testis or fertility concerns. A new varicocele on the right, one on both sides that appeared suddenly, or one that does not empty when the patient lies down, can come from a mass pressing on the veins higher up: arrange imaging of the abdomen and kidneys.

?? Why are most varicoceles on the left?
+ The left testicular vein drains at a right angle into the left renal vein
- The left testis is larger
- The right side has no veins
- Patients lie on their left side
: The drainage anatomy raises pressure in the left testicular vein.

?? Which manoeuvre makes a varicocele easier to see?
+ Valsalva or standing
- Lying flat and breathing quietly
- Pressing hard with the probe
- Cooling the scrotum
: Raised abdominal pressure fills the dilated veins and reverses flow.

?? About what vein size supports a varicocele?
+ Over about 3 mm, enlarging on Valsalva
- Under 1 mm
- Exactly 10 mm
- Size is never measured
: Size and dynamic change are judged together.

?? A man has a new right-sided varicocele that does not empty lying down. What next?
+ Imaging of the abdomen and kidneys for a mass pressing on the veins
- Reassure and discharge
- Urgent scrotal exploration
- Repeat the scan in a year
: An isolated right or non-decompressing varicocele can be secondary.

?? Why compare the size of both testes?
+ A smaller testis on the varicocele side matters for referral
- To exclude appendicitis
- To measure blood pressure
- It is not useful
: Testicular size affects fertility advice and referral.
