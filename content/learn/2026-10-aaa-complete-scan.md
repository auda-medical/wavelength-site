Bowel gas, body habitus and pain all make the abdominal aorta difficult to see. The scan only rules out an aneurysm when you see the whole length.

![Wavelength Pearl: abdominal aorta. A transverse sweep from the epigastrium to the umbilicus, the aorta in front of the spine with the IVC beside it, the coeliac axis and SMA, an aneurysm measured outer wall to outer wall, and the bifurcation.](/learn/images/aaa-complete-pearl.webp)

*Scan images in the graphic are illustrations, not patient scans.*

## The complete scan

1. Start in the epigastrium in transverse, with the curvilinear probe. Find the vertebral body, then the aorta sitting just to the left of the midline, in front of it.
2. Follow the aorta down in transverse, past the coeliac axis and the superior mesenteric artery.
3. Continue to the bifurcation into the common iliac arteries, usually around the level of the umbilicus.
4. Measure the largest diameter in transverse, from outer wall to outer wall, at a right angle to the vessel.
5. Confirm in longitudinal.

## Interpretation

An abdominal aorta of 3 cm or more is aneurysmal. Measure outer wall to outer wall, because thrombus inside the lumen leads to underestimation when you measure the lumen alone.

> **Pitfall** If you cannot see a segment, record the scan as incomplete. Steady pressure, moving the probe to the side, or asking the patient to bend the knees often clears bowel gas. A patient with suspected rupture who is unstable needs a senior and a vascular surgeon, not a perfect image.

?? Bowel gas hides the lower aorta, but the upper part looks normal. How do you record the scan?
+ Incomplete
- Normal
- Aneurysm excluded
- Aneurysm present
: A scan rules out an aneurysm only when you have seen the whole length.

?? Where does a complete aortic scan run?
+ From just below the diaphragm to the bifurcation into the common iliac arteries
- From the heart to the kidneys
- Only at the level of the umbilicus
- From the renal arteries to the groin
: Follow it down in transverse past the coeliac axis and the superior mesenteric artery to the bifurcation.

?? What diameter of the abdominal aorta counts as aneurysmal?
+ 3 cm or more
- 2 cm or more
- 4 cm or more
- 5.5 cm or more
: 5.5 cm is a threshold for elective repair, not for calling an aneurysm.

?? How do you measure the aorta?
+ Outer wall to outer wall, at a right angle to the vessel, at its widest
- Inside the lumen only, where the blood flows
- Along its length in longitudinal
- Obliquely, where it looks largest
: Thrombus lines many aneurysms. Measuring the lumen alone underestimates the size.

?? A patient with a known aneurysm is hypotensive with back pain. What matters most?
+ A senior and a vascular surgeon, now
- A complete, perfect scan before anyone is called
- A CT scan before the surgeon is told
- Repeat measurements to confirm the size
: Suspected rupture in an unstable patient is a call for help, not a reason to perfect the image.
