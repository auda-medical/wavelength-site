Odontogenic infection is a common cause of facial swelling in the emergency department. The question is whether there is pus to drain or cellulitis to treat with antibiotics and dental care. Ultrasound over the swelling answers it in the same way as an [abscess](/learn/abscess-ultrasound/) elsewhere: look for a pocket, press for the swirl and check the flow.

![Wavelength Pearl: dental swelling. Cheek soft tissue over the alveolar bone, oedema without a collection, and a focal pocket beside the bone with flow in its rim.](/learn/images/dental-abscess-pearl.webp)

*Scan images in the graphic are illustrations, not patient scans.*

## The scan

1. Use a high-frequency linear probe on the cheek over the most tender, swollen point.
2. Find the bright surface of the alveolar bone of the jaw, with its shadow.
3. Sweep across the swelling in two planes, from the skin down to the bone.
4. Press gently: pus may swirl.
5. Add colour Doppler and compare with the other cheek.

## Reading the view

- **Cellulitis:** thickened, brighter fat with dark strands between the fat lobules (cobblestoning) and no discrete pocket.
- **Collection:** a hypoechoic or anechoic pocket, often lying against the bone surface, that may swirl when pressed.
- **Doppler:** flow in the inflamed rim and none in the centre.
- **Bone:** nothing deep to the bright cortex can be seen, so a collection inside the bone or behind it is invisible.

> **The aha** The pocket sits on the bone, not under it.

> **Pitfall** A superficial scan does not exclude deep-space infection. Trismus, drooling, a change in voice, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or swelling of the floor of the mouth suggest spread such as Ludwig's angina: airway first, senior help, CT and maxillofacial surgery.

## Action

Treat the source. A collection is drained, often by the dental or maxillofacial team, along with treatment of the tooth. Cellulitis without a pocket needs antibiotics and dental follow-up, with a plan to rescan if it does not settle.

?? What does cellulitis without a collection look like?
+ Thickened, brighter fat with dark strands between lobules and no discrete pocket
- A round anechoic pocket with a thick rim
- A bright line with a shadow
- Normal thin tissue
: Cobblestoning is oedema, not pus.

?? Where does a vestibular collection usually lie?
+ Against the alveolar bone, superficial to its bright surface
- Inside the bone's acoustic shadow
- In the parotid duct
- Only deep in the neck
: Ultrasound cannot see beneath bone.

?? What does colour Doppler usually show around an abscess?
+ Flow in the inflamed rim and none in the centre
- Strong flow throughout the pocket
- Arterial flow within the fluid
- No flow anywhere nearby
: Pus has no flow.

?? Which finding puts the airway before more scanning?
+ Trismus, drooling, voice change or floor-of-mouth swelling
- A tender upper canine
- Swelling for one day
- A normal temperature
: These suggest deep-space infection such as Ludwig's angina.

?? Who treats the source of a dental abscess?
+ Dental or maxillofacial services
- Ophthalmology
- Dermatology alone
- No one: antibiotics are always enough
: Draining pus and treating the tooth control the infection.
