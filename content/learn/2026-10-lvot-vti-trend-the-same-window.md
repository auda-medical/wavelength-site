The left ventricular outflow tract velocity time integral (LVOT VTI) is the distance a column of blood travels through the outflow tract in one beat. If the outflow tract stays the same size, a change in VTI reflects a change in stroke volume. Its strength is repetition. Its weakness is comparing two measurements which were taken differently.

![Wavelength Pearl: LVOT VTI. Apical five-chamber view, the pulsed-wave gate just below the aortic valve, a traced envelope, and the same window before and after.](/learn/images/lvot-vti-pearl.webp)

*Scan images in the graphic are fabricated educational illustrations, not patient scans.*

## The scan

1. Get the best apical five-chamber or apical long-axis view you can reproduce.
2. Line the Doppler beam up with the flow through the outflow tract.
3. Place the pulsed-wave gate in the outflow tract, just below the aortic valve, away from the acceleration at the valve itself.
4. Record several clean envelopes. Trace the outer edge of the dense part of the signal, the same way every time.
5. Average beats: at least three in a regular rhythm, more in an irregular one.
6. After a fluid bolus, a passive leg raise or a drug, return to the same window, depth, gate position and tracing method.

## Reading the trend

- A clearly larger repeat VTI, taken the same way, supports a rise in stroke volume.
- Record the heart rate too. Cardiac output is stroke volume times heart rate.
- One value is not a fluid prescription. Read it with the circulation, the rhythm, the valves and ventricular function.

> **The aha** Repeatability is the measurement. If the gate moved, the comparison changed.

> **Pitfall** Aortic stenosis, outflow tract obstruction, significant aortic regurgitation, arrhythmia and poor alignment all make the simple reading unreliable.

## Action

Use VTI as one part of a focused haemodynamic reassessment. Write down the window and settings so the next measurement is truly comparable.

?? What makes two LVOT VTI measurements comparable?
+ The same window, gate position and tracing method
- The same machine brand only
- One beat before and one after treatment
- Any apical view which shows the ventricle
: Small changes in alignment or gate position change the envelope, whatever the physiology is doing.

?? If the outflow tract stays the same size, what does VTI mainly track?
+ Stroke volume
- Left atrial pressure
- Ejection fraction only
- Systemic vascular resistance
: VTI is the distance the blood travels with each beat. Multiplied by the outflow tract area, it estimates stroke volume.

?? Why record the heart rate alongside VTI?
+ Cardiac output depends on both stroke volume and heart rate
- Heart rate changes the outflow tract diameter at once
- VTI cannot be measured below 100 beats a minute
- The ECG is needed only to store the image
: A higher stroke volume with a much lower heart rate may not mean a higher cardiac output.

?? How should you measure VTI in atrial fibrillation?
+ Average several representative beats
- Trace the tallest beat only
- Use one beat at random
- No change is needed, as VTI is exact in atrial fibrillation
: Filling varies from beat to beat, so a single envelope is unreliable.

?? VTI rises after a passive leg raise. How should you describe this?
+ Supportive evidence of a rise in stroke volume, read with the whole assessment
- Proof that the patient needs a fluid bolus
- Proof of normal ventricular function
- A direct measure of blood pressure
: VTI adds haemodynamic information. It is not a treatment instruction on its own.
