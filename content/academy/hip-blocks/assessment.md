?? What does a certificate for this module show?
@ 01-context
+ That you have completed the theory, not that you are competent to perform the blocks
- That you can perform the blocks without supervision
- That your trust has approved you for regional anaesthesia
- That you hold a national accreditation in nerve blocks
: Competence comes from supervised practice under your trust's governance.

?? Which outcome did a Cochrane review find with nerve blocks for hip fracture?
@ 01-context
+ A lower risk of acute confusion
- A higher risk of chest infection
- No effect on pain on movement
- A higher risk of death
: The review found less pain on movement, and lower risks of acute confusion and chest infection.

?? Which nerves supply the front of the hip capsule?
@ 01-context
+ The femoral, obturator and accessory obturator nerves
- The sciatic nerve alone
- The lateral femoral cutaneous nerve alone
- The pudendal nerve
: The front of the capsule receives branches from these three nerves. The back receives branches from the sciatic nerve and the nerve to quadratus femoris.

?? What is the initial dose of 20% lipid emulsion in local anaesthetic systemic toxicity?
@ 02-safety
+ A bolus of 1.5 mL/kg over 1 minute, then an infusion of 15 mL/kg/h
- A bolus of 10 mL/kg over 10 minutes
- 50 mL over 30 minutes for every patient
- An infusion of 1 mL/kg/h with no bolus
: Up to two further boluses can follow at 5-minute intervals, with the infusion doubled to 30 mL/kg/h, to a cumulative maximum of 12 mL/kg.

?? When does the Stop Before You Block check take place?
@ 02-safety
+ Immediately before the needle touches the skin
- At the start of the consent discussion
- When the patient arrives in the department
- After the injection, before documentation
: The final check of the side happens with the needle in your hand.

?? At the inguinal crease, what is the order of structures from lateral to medial?
@ 03-femoral
+ Nerve, artery, vein
- Vein, artery, nerve
- Artery, nerve, vein
- Artery, vein, nerve
: The femoral nerve lies lateral to the artery, outside the femoral sheath, deep to fascia iliaca.

?? Which injury is a femoral nerve block well suited to?
@ 03-femoral
+ A femoral shaft fracture in a child
- A fracture of the distal radius
- A rib fracture
- An ankle fracture
: The femoral nerve supplies most of the femur. A femoral block gives fast analgesia for a shaft fracture.

?? What must you tell the ward team after a femoral nerve block?
@ 03-femoral
+ The quadriceps will be weak, so the patient must not stand on the leg until the block wears off
- The patient can walk straight away
- The patient must fast for 24 hours
- The block will last a week
: Quadriceps weakness can cause a fall.

?? Why is the dose the main safety concern in a fascia iliaca block?
@ 04-fascia-iliaca
+ The block uses 30 to 40 mL, which brings the total dose close to the maximum for a frail patient
- The needle always enters the femoral artery
- Local anaesthetic does not spread under fascia iliaca
- The block needs adrenaline
: Volume blocks bring large doses. Calculate the maximum safe dose for the patient's weight first.

?? What confirms the correct plane in an infra-inguinal fascia iliaca block?
@ 04-fascia-iliaca
+ Fluid separating fascia iliaca from iliacus and spreading along it
- A swelling inside the femoral nerve
- Fluid around sartorius above the fascia
- A pop felt with no fluid seen
: The aim is to see the fascia lift off iliacus. Fluid above the fascia will not reach the nerves.

?? Which probe orientation does the supra-inguinal fascia iliaca block use?
@ 04-fascia-iliaca
+ Sagittal, medial to the anterior superior iliac spine, above the inguinal ligament
- Transverse below the inguinal ligament
- Over the greater trochanter
- Parasagittal over the lumbar spine
: The sagittal view shows iliacus, fascia iliaca and the spread towards the head.

?? Where does the needle tip sit in a PENG block?
@ 05-peng
+ On the superior pubic ramus, under the psoas tendon, near the iliopubic eminence
- Inside the femoral nerve
- Beside the sciatic nerve
- Under fascia iliaca lateral to sartorius
: The articular branches run between the psoas tendon and the ramus. Fluid there lifts the tendon off the bone.

?? Which area does a PENG block not cover?
@ 05-peng
+ The skin of the lateral thigh
- The front of the hip capsule
- The articular branches of the femoral nerve
- The articular branches of the accessory obturator nerve
: The PENG block covers the front of the capsule. The lateral thigh skin belongs to the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve.

?? Which block most often spares the quadriceps?
@ 05-peng
+ The PENG block
- The femoral nerve block
- The infra-inguinal fascia iliaca block
- The supra-inguinal fascia iliaca block
: The PENG block targets the articular branches rather than the main femoral nerve, though high volumes can still spread to it.
