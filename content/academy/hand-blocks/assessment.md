?? What does a certificate for this module show?
@ 01-context
+ That you have completed the theory, not that you are competent to perform the blocks
- That you can perform the blocks without supervision
- That your trust has approved you for regional anaesthesia
- That you hold a national accreditation in nerve blocks
: The module teaches the safety system, the anatomy, the needle path and the evidence. Competence comes from supervised practice under your trust's governance.

?? What must you record before a block for a hand wound?
@ 01-context
+ Sensation in each nerve territory, two-point discrimination, tendon function and circulation
- Only the time of injury
- Only the patient's weight
- Nothing until the block has worked
: A block removes sensation. Document the examination first so a nerve or tendon injury is not missed or blamed on the block.

?? Which nerve supplies the back of the thumb and the first web space?
@ 01-context
+ The superficial radial nerve
- The median nerve
- The ulnar nerve
- The palmar cutaneous branch of the median nerve
: The superficial branch of the radial nerve supplies the radial side of the back of the hand, the back of the thumb and the first web space.

?? When does the Stop Before You Block check take place?
@ 02-safety
+ Immediately before the needle touches the skin
- At the start of the consent discussion
- When the patient arrives in the department
- After the injection, before documentation
: Wrong-side blocks often follow a distraction between the earlier checks and the needle. The final check happens with the needle in your hand.

?? Which group of symptoms suggests early local anaesthetic systemic toxicity?
@ 02-safety
+ Tingling around the mouth, a metallic taste and ringing in the ears
- Numbness in the territory of the block
- Itching at the injection site
- A brief sting during skin infiltration
: Early toxicity affects the brain. Seizures and cardiovascular collapse can follow, sometimes with no warning.

?? Where does the median nerve run in the mid forearm?
@ 03-median
+ Between flexor digitorum superficialis and flexor digitorum profundus
- Beside the radial artery under brachioradialis
- Beside the ulnar artery under flexor carpi ulnaris
- Over the extensor tendons
: The median nerve runs between the two finger flexor muscles, moving superficial close to the wrist.

?? How do you tell the median nerve from a flexor tendon on ultrasound?
@ 03-median
+ Slide up the forearm: the tendon joins its muscle while the nerve keeps its honeycomb look
- The nerve pulsates
- The tendon is always deeper than the bone
- The nerve shows flow on colour Doppler
: Tendons turn into muscle as you move up. The nerve continues unchanged. Tendons also darken more with probe tilt.

?? During injection beside the median nerve, the nerve swells and the patient reports electric pain. What do you do?
@ 03-median
+ Stop, withdraw slightly and reposition
- Continue, as this confirms the right place
- Inject faster to finish quickly
- Double the volume
: Nerve swelling and electric pain suggest an intraneural injection. Stop at once and reposition.

?? Where does the ulnar nerve lie in the distal forearm?
@ 04-ulnar-radial
+ On the ulnar side of the ulnar artery, under flexor carpi ulnaris
- On the radial side of the radial artery
- Between the finger flexors with the median nerve
- On the back of the radius
: The nerve runs beside the ulnar artery on its ulnar side, covered by flexor carpi ulnaris.

?? Why can a distal ulnar block miss a wound on the back of the ulnar hand?
@ 04-ulnar-radial
+ The dorsal cutaneous branch leaves the nerve about 5 cm above the wrist
- The ulnar nerve does not reach the hand
- The radial nerve supplies the whole back of the hand
- The ulnar artery blocks the spread
: Block higher in the forearm or add a field block to reach the dorsal cutaneous branch.

?? Where does the superficial radial nerve lie in the mid forearm?
@ 04-ulnar-radial
+ On the radial side of the radial artery, under brachioradialis
- Between flexor digitorum superficialis and profundus
- On the ulnar side of the ulnar artery
- In the carpal tunnel
: In the mid forearm the nerve runs beside the radial artery under brachioradialis, before becoming subcutaneous above the wrist.

?? Where do the palmar digital nerves run at the base of the finger?
@ 05-digital
+ On each side of the flexor tendon sheath, beside the digital arteries
- Inside the flexor tendon sheath
- Over the extensor tendon
- Through the proximal phalanx
: Each palmar digital nerve runs with its artery on one side of the flexor sheath. Each needs its own deposit.

?? What does the evidence show about lidocaine with adrenaline in the fingers of healthy patients?
@ 05-digital
+ A large multicentre study found no finger loss
- It causes finger necrosis in one in ten patients
- It is banned in UK practice
- It is safe only in the thumb
: The Dalhousie Project studied 3,110 procedures with adrenaline in the hand and fingers and found no finger loss. Avoid it where circulation is already poor.

?? Why do you avoid a large volume around the whole base of a finger?
@ 05-digital
+ It raises the pressure in a closed space and can compromise the circulation
- It makes the block wear off faster
- It numbs the whole hand
- It always enters the flexor sheath
: Small paired deposits beside each nerve give a good block without high pressure.
