Five patients from the emergency department. Read each case, choose your answer, then read the explanation. These cases do not count towards your score.

## Case 1: the glass laceration

A 28-year-old chef has a deep 4 cm laceration across the thenar eminence from broken glass. You plan to explore and close it.

?? What do you do before any block?
+ Record sensation in each nerve territory, two-point discrimination and the function of each tendon
- Perform the block, then examine the hand once it is numb
- X-ray the hand and block without examining
- Give intravenous morphine and examine the next day
: A block removes sensation. Document nerve and tendon function first, so an injury is not missed or attributed to the block.

## Case 2: the thenar wound

After examination, the nerve and tendon function are normal. The wound lies over the thenar eminence.

?? Where do you place the median nerve block?
+ In the mid to distal forearm, above the origin of the palmar cutaneous branch
- At the wrist crease
- At the base of the thumb
- In the upper arm
: The palmar cutaneous branch leaves above the wrist and supplies the skin over the thenar eminence. A wrist block can miss it.

## Case 3: the boxer's knuckle

A 22-year-old man has a laceration over the back of the fifth metacarpal head after a fight.

?? Which plan covers the wound?
+ An ulnar block higher in the forearm, or a field block over the ulnar side of the back of the hand
- A distal ulnar block at the wrist alone
- A median nerve block
- A superficial radial nerve block
: The dorsal cutaneous branch of the ulnar nerve leaves about 5 cm above the wrist. A higher block or a field block reaches it. Remember to look for a fight bite into the joint.

## Case 4: the nail bed

A 9-year-old has a crush injury of the middle finger with a nail bed laceration. A parent is present, and the child agrees to a block.

?? Which block suits this injury?
+ A digital block with a small deposit beside each palmar digital nerve
- A superficial radial nerve block
- An ulnar nerve block
- A single large injection around the whole base of the finger
: The palmar digital nerves supply the back of the middle finger beyond the middle joint, including the nail bed. Small paired deposits avoid high pressure around the base of the finger.

## Case 5: one side still hurts

After a digital block of the ring finger, the radial side is numb but the ulnar side remains painful 15 minutes later.

?? What do you do?
+ Give a small deposit beside the ulnar digital nerve
- Repeat both sides with a larger volume
- Abandon the block and use sedation
- Wait another hour
: Each side has its own nerve. A painful side means that nerve was missed. Check the total dose before you inject again.
