?? Why is ultrasound more useful than examination for a red, swollen area?
@ 01-context
+ Examination often misses deep or small collections, and ultrasound shows whether there is anything to drain
- Ultrasound diagnoses the causative organism
- Fluctuance is always present in an abscess
- Ultrasound replaces the need for antibiotics
: Fluctuance is unreliable, especially in deep, small or indurated collections. A 2020 meta-analysis found ultrasound diagnosed abscess with a sensitivity of about 95%, outperforming examination alone.

?? Which patient's groin lump needs colour Doppler before any needle?
@ 01-context
+ Every patient with a groin lump
- Only patients on anticoagulants
- Only patients with a bruit
- Only patients after angiography
: Any groin lump sits close to the femoral artery. Pseudoaneurysms occur after puncture, after injecting drug use and after trauma, and they do not always pulsate or have a bruit. Colour Doppler takes seconds.

?? Why do you scan normal skin next to the swelling first?
@ 02-technique
+ To see what normal fat and fascia look like in this patient
- To warm the gel
- To calibrate colour Doppler
- To measure the swelling
: Fat thickness and brightness vary between patients and areas. Scanning normal tissue first gives you a baseline, so you can judge whether the tissue over the swelling is abnormal.

?? How do you set colour Doppler before calling no flow inside a collection?
@ 02-technique
+ Lower the scale so it detects slow flow
- Raise the scale to the maximum
- Switch to the curvilinear probe
- Press firmly with the probe
: A high colour scale misses slow flow, such as in a partly clotted pseudoaneurysm sac. A low scale picks it up. Firm pressure can stop slow flow altogether.

?? What does the cobblestone pattern in subcutaneous fat show?
@ 03-cellulitis-abscess
+ Oedema between fat lobules, as seen in cellulitis
- A drainable collection
- A lymph node
- A foreign body
: Dark strands of fluid between thickened fat lobules make the cobblestone pattern. It fits cellulitis, but heart failure, lymphoedema and venous disease cause it too. It is not a collection.

?? A collection looks grey and solid on a still image. Which manoeuvre shows it is liquid?
@ 03-cellulitis-abscess
+ Gentle compression with the probe, watching the contents move
- Increasing the gain
- Switching to the curvilinear probe
- Scanning in one plane only
: Thick pus looks solid on a still image. Gentle pressure and release makes liquid contents swirl. This separates an abscess from solid tissue or phlegmon.

?? Which ultrasound feature suggests necrotising fasciitis?
@ 03-cellulitis-abscess
+ Fluid along the deep fascia with bright foci and dirty shadowing from gas
- A well-defined collection with a bright rim
- A lymph node with a fatty hilum
- Cobblestoning confined to the fat
: Fluid tracking along the deep fascia and gas in the tissues suggest necrotising infection, a surgical emergency. Ultrasound does not exclude it, so clinical suspicion drives urgent surgical review.

?? What finding confirms a pseudoaneurysm?
@ 04-vascular-lumps
+ A to-and-fro waveform in a neck connecting the sac to the artery
- A round collection near the artery
- Clot inside a collection
- Swirling flow in the sac without a neck
: The to-and-fro waveform shows flow into the sac in systole and back to the artery in diastole through the neck. Swirl shows moving blood but not the connection. Clot occurs in haematomas and in partly thrombosed sacs.

?? In the right groin, what is the order of structures from medial to lateral?
@ 04-vascular-lumps
+ Femoral vein, femoral artery, femoral nerve
- Femoral artery, femoral vein, femoral nerve
- Femoral nerve, femoral artery, femoral vein
- Femoral vein, femoral nerve, femoral artery
: From medial to lateral: vein, artery, nerve. Find the artery in transverse, with the vein medial to it, then follow it to the swelling.

?? How does a true aneurysm differ from a pseudoaneurysm on ultrasound?
@ 04-vascular-lumps
+ The artery itself is wider, with its own wall all the way round and no separate neck
- A true aneurysm shows to-and-fro flow in a neck
- A true aneurysm never contains clot
- A true aneurysm shows no flow on colour Doppler
: A true aneurysm is a widening of the whole artery. A pseudoaneurysm is a leak through the wall, held by surrounding tissue, joined to the artery by a narrow neck. Both can contain clot and both show flow.

?? Which sign makes a groin pseudoaneurysm urgent?
@ 04-vascular-lumps
+ A cold, pale or numb leg
- A sac smaller than 2 cm
- Clot lining part of the sac
- A long, narrow neck
: Limb ischaemia, rapid growth, skin necrosis and active bleeding all need urgent vascular review. A small sac with a long, narrow neck is often suitable for thrombin injection or observation.

?? Before draining an abscess, what do you check with colour Doppler?
@ 05-safe-drainage
+ No flow inside the collection and no vessel in the planned path
- The speed of blood in the nearest artery
- The temperature of the skin
- The thickness of the skin
: Flow inside a collection means it is not a simple abscess. Vessels in the needle or blade path risk bleeding. Colour Doppler over and around the collection takes seconds and prevents both.

?? Ultrasound shows phlegmon with no drainable collection. What is the best plan?
@ 05-safe-drainage
+ Antibiotics and a repeat scan if it does not settle
- Incision at the point of maximum tenderness
- Needle aspiration of the phlegmon
- Discharge with no follow-up
: Phlegmon is inflamed tissue without a collection. Incision finds nothing to drain. A collection can form over 24 to 48 hours, so rescan if the area does not improve.

?? Which approach lets you see the whole needle during ultrasound-guided aspiration?
@ 05-safe-drainage
+ In-plane, with the needle running along the length of the probe
- Out-of-plane, with the needle crossing the beam
- Landmark technique after marking
- Blind aspiration at the point of maximum fluctuance
: In-plane guidance shows the full length of the needle, including the tip, as it enters the collection. Out-of-plane shows only a dot where the needle crosses the beam, which can be the shaft rather than the tip.

?? A layered collection appears in the thigh of a patient on an anticoagulant after a fall. Colour Doppler, with the scale set low, shows no flow inside and no connection to a vessel. What is it?
@ 04-vascular-lumps
+ A haematoma
- A pseudoaneurysm
- An arteriovenous fistula
- A true aneurysm
: A haematoma is blood outside the vessels, with no flow inside and no connection to an artery. A pseudoaneurysm, a fistula and a true aneurysm all show flow. Monitor its size and the skin over it.
