## Cellulitis

- Thickened subcutaneous fat, brighter than the normal side.
- Dark strands of oedema running between the fat lobules, making a cobblestone pattern.
- No discrete collection.

Cobblestoning is not specific to infection. Heart failure, lymphoedema and venous disease cause it too. Compare with the other side and with the clinical picture.

## Abscess

- A dark or mixed collection, often irregular, with brighter debris inside.
- A brighter, thickened rim of inflamed tissue around it.
- Posterior acoustic enhancement: the tissue behind looks brighter than the tissue beside it.
- Flow around the collection on colour Doppler, none inside.

## Make it swirl

An abscess full of thick pus looks grey and solid on a still image. Press gently with the probe and release. If the contents move or swirl, they are liquid. This is the most useful single sign to separate an abscess from a solid lump or phlegmon.

> **The aha** Still images lie. Movement under gentle pressure proves fluid.

## Phlegmon

Inflamed, swollen tissue without a drainable collection. It looks like a cobblestoned area with a vaguely darker centre, which does not swirl. It needs antibiotics and a repeat scan, not an incision.

## Other mimics

- **Lymph node:** oval, dark rim, bright fatty centre (the hilum), flow entering at the hilum.
- **Epidermoid cyst:** well-defined, often with an onion-ring or speckled pattern, sometimes a punctum to the skin.
- **Foreign body:** a bright line or shape with a shadow or reverberation, often with fluid around it.
- **Necrotising fasciitis:** fluid along the deep fascia, thickened fat, and bright foci with dirty shadowing from gas. A surgical emergency. Ultrasound does not exclude it.

## The finger: fluid in the flexor sheath

A red, swollen finger can be cellulitis, a superficial abscess or infection inside the flexor tendon sheath. Follow the flexor tendon from the fingertip into the palm. Fluid surrounding the tendon within a distended sheath supports flexor tenosynovitis, a hand surgery emergency; fluid above a normal sheath is a superficial collection. The Kanavel signs decide, and a scan with little fluid does not exclude infection. See [Finger infection: fluid in the sheath](/learn/flexor-tenosynovitis-ultrasound/).

## Neck lumps: node or pus

In the neck, the commonest tender lump is an inflamed lymph node. Look for the bright hilum before you call a collection. A round lump with no hilum, debris and posterior enhancement suggests an abscess or a liquefying node, common in children. In an adult, a round or cystic node without a hilum may be a metastasis, and needs your local urgent suspected cancer pathway, not an incision. Stridor, trismus, drooling or difficulty swallowing suggest a deep neck infection, which ultrasound cannot see: call ENT. The Wavelength Pearl [Neck lump: hilum or pus?](/learn/neck-lump-node-or-abscess-ultrasound/) compares the patterns.

## Dental swelling: the vestibular pocket

Facial swelling from a tooth follows the same rules. Over the swelling, find the bright surface of the jaw bone: a collection is a dark pocket lying against it, superficial to the bone, with flow in its rim. Oedematous fat without a pocket is cellulitis. Ultrasound cannot see the deep neck spaces: trismus, drooling, a change in voice or swelling of the floor of the mouth put the airway first. The Wavelength Pearl [Dental swelling: find the vestibular pocket](/learn/dental-abscess-ultrasound/) shows each step.

## Pain deep in the muscle: pyomyositis

When the pain is deep and constant, scan below the fascia. Pyomyositis is infection inside the muscle: first a swollen muscle that has lost its striations, then a complex pocket within it, with flow in the rim. It affects people with diabetes, immunosuppression or injecting drug use, and children. Early disease can look almost normal, so MRI and surgical review follow when the picture fits. The Wavelength Pearl [Pyomyositis: look below the fascia](/learn/pyomyositis-ultrasound/) compares it with cellulitis.

> **Pitfall** Swirl tells you the lump holds liquid. Blood moves too. Switch on colour Doppler before any needle or blade.
