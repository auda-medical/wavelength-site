## The femoral anatomy

In the groin, from medial to lateral: the common femoral vein, the common femoral artery, then the femoral nerve. Find the artery in transverse first, then follow it to the swelling.

## Haematoma

A collection of blood outside the vessels.

- Early: dark or mixed, often with layers. Later: clot gives a grey, solid look, then becomes dark again as it liquefies.
- No flow inside on colour Doppler, with the scale set low.
- No connection to the artery.

## Pseudoaneurysm

A contained leak from a hole in the artery wall. Blood flows out through a short channel, the neck, into a sac walled by surrounding tissue, then back again.

- **The sac:** swirling flow in two directions, red and blue side by side, the yin-yang sign. Clot often lines part of it.
- **The neck:** a narrow channel joining the artery to the sac.
- **The waveform:** pulsed-wave Doppler in the neck shows flow into the sac in systole, above the baseline, and back to the artery in diastole, below it. This to-and-fro pattern confirms the diagnosis.

> **The aha** The sac catches your eye. The neck makes the diagnosis.

## True aneurysm

The artery itself is wider, with its own wall all the way round. There is no separate neck. Flow is often turbulent, and clot can line the wall.

## Arteriovenous fistula

A direct connection between artery and vein after a puncture through both. The vein shows high-velocity, pulsatile, arterial-looking flow, and colour Doppler shows aliasing at the connection.

## Referral

Refer every pseudoaneurysm to the vascular or interventional radiology team. Formal duplex defines its size, neck length and treatment. Options include ultrasound-guided thrombin injection, guided compression and surgical repair. Some small pseudoaneurysms close by themselves under follow-up.

Urgent signs:

- A rapidly growing or very painful mass.
- Skin changes or necrosis over it.
- Active bleeding.
- A cold, pale or numb leg.

> **Pitfall** In people who inject drugs, an abscess and a pseudoaneurysm often sit together. Infected pseudoaneurysms rupture. Scan before you cut, every time.
