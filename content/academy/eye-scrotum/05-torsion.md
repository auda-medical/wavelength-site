Testicular torsion twists the spermatic cord and cuts off the blood supply. The chance of saving the testis falls with every hour. Torsion is a clinical diagnosis. A strong suspicion goes straight to urology, without waiting for a scan.

## Torsion

- **The whirlpool sign:** in short axis, the twisted cord forms a round mass with vessels spiralling into it, like a snail shell. In a 2018 meta-analysis by McDowall and colleagues, the whirlpool sign had a pooled sensitivity of 73% and a specificity of 99%. When present it strongly supports torsion. When absent it does not exclude it.
- **Reduced or absent flow** in the painful testis compared with the other side.
- **An enlarged testis** which becomes darker and uneven as it infarcts.
- **A horizontal lie** and a thickened, swollen cord.

## Epididymo-orchitis

- An enlarged epididymis with increased colour flow compared with the other side.
- The testis may also show increased flow and swelling.
- Often a reactive hydrocele and thickened scrotal skin.
- A cord without a twist.

## Torsion of a testicular appendage

A small round structure at the upper pole, often with no flow and a bright rim, in a testis with normal flow. Common in boys aged 7 to 12. A "blue dot" may show through the skin. Reactive fluid and a thick scrotal wall can make it look dramatic, so still follow the cord. The Wavelength Pearl [Appendix testis torsion: find the tender nub](/learn/appendix-testis-torsion-ultrasound/) shows each step.

> **The aha** If you suspect torsion, follow the cord, not just the colour box.

> **Pitfall** Never delay urology review for a scan. Partial or intermittent torsion preserves flow, and a testis which has spontaneously untwisted looks hyperaemic, like epididymo-orchitis. Normal colour Doppler does not exclude torsion.

## Fluid around the testis

A simple hydrocele is black fluid around the testis. Debris, strands or septations suggest a pyocele with infection, or a haematocele after trauma. The fluid is a window, not the diagnosis: look at the testis inside it. After trauma, a broken testicular outline or a disorganised testis suggests rupture, which needs urgent urology review. A new hydrocele in a young man can hide a tumour. A small reactive hydrocele often accompanies torsion and infection. The Wavelength Pearl [Scrotal fluid: clear or complex?](/learn/hydrocele-scrotal-fluid-ultrasound/) shows the patterns.

## Varicocele

A cluster of dilated veins above the testis, usually on the left, which enlarge and fill with reversed colour flow when the patient bears down or stands. Veins over about 3 mm support it. Most are routine referrals. A new right-sided varicocele, or one that does not empty lying down, can come from a mass pressing on the veins higher up: image the abdomen and kidneys. ## Who goes straight to urology

Sudden severe pain, nausea and vomiting, a high-riding or horizontal testis, an absent cremasteric reflex, or a history of similar episodes which settled. Call urology on clinical grounds. A bedside scan may be done while they come, but not instead.
