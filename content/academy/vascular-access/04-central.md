This lesson covers one safety check in internal jugular vein cannulation. It does not teach the whole procedure.

## Before you start

Central venous catheter insertion needs consent, full sterile precautions, a trained operator or direct supervision, and your trust's policy. Scan the neck before you prepare the skin.

## Know the neighbours

Scan the neck in short axis. The internal jugular vein usually lies lateral to the common carotid artery. The vein compresses with gentle pressure, varies in size with breathing, and the artery pulsates and does not compress. Note any overlap of the vein over the artery, because a needle that passes through the vein can reach the artery behind it.

## Real-time guidance

Use real-time ultrasound, with the needle tip in view, for internal jugular vein access. The 2019 Society of Hospital Medicine position statement recommends it, because it reduces mechanical and infectious complications, and accepts either a short-axis or a long-axis approach.

## See the wire in two views

After you pass the guidewire:

1. **Short axis.** Place the probe over the vein. The wire should lie clearly inside the black lumen, as a bright dot or short line.
2. **Long axis.** Turn the probe and follow the wire along the vein. It should run within the lumen, and not in the tissue beside it.
3. **Decide.** If the wire is not clearly in the vein, stop and recheck. Dilate only after you have seen it.

The same statement recommends that you see the needle tip and the guidewire in the target vein before you dilate.

## Why not trust the flash

Flashback and easy wire passage do not exclude arterial or extraluminal placement. Arterial blood can return without a pulsatile jet, especially in a hypotensive or hypoxic patient, and a wire can pass through the vein wall. A large dilator in the carotid artery is a major injury.

## What the wire view does not show

A wire inside the vein at the neck does not show where the catheter tip will lie. After insertion, confirm tip position and look for complications such as pneumothorax, in line with your trust's policy.

> **The aha** Hope is not wire confirmation. Two views, then dilate.
