# Store badges

Official artwork, unmodified (owner approved the download, 20 Oct 2026):

- `google-play-badge-en.png`: Google Play badge, from https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png. Use with the line "Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC."
- `app-store-badge-en-gb.svg`: Apple "Download on the App Store" badge, black, English (UK), from https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/en-gb. Show it only once the app is on the App Store, at least 40 px high, with "Apple and the Apple logo are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries. App Store is a service mark of Apple Inc."

Both are shown on /app/get/ (build.js, STORES).
