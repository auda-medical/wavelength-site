## How it works
- Static site, no framework. `node build.js` builds `dist/` from `data/*.json` and `src/`.
- Cloudflare Workers (static assets) builds every push to `main`: build `npm run build`, deploy `npx wrangler deploy` using `wrangler.jsonc`. Pushing to `main` publishes the live site.
- Domain: thewavelength.co.uk (and www), attached as custom domains in `wrangler.jsonc` routes and set in `data/site.json` (`domain`). workers.dev is off so only the real domain is indexed. Firas is bidding for wavelength.co.uk; if he wins, change `domain` there.

- International pathways (October 2026): RCEM, FAMUS, FUSIC, EUSEM basic and advanced (Core Compendium, Ultraschall in Med 2026: topics in two tiers, no universal numbers, so modules count assessments and learners set their own targets), ACEM (P733 v6.2, Dec 2025), CPoCUS Acute Care CORE (50 supervised determinate scans; per-area split is members-only) and ACEP (25 per core application benchmark). Rules support `match`, `any` (one of several) and `not`; modules support `filter` (EUSEM paediatrics counts Under 16 only), `topics` and `assessments` (counted by kind and outcome).
- One progress engine: `src/app/progress.js` (window.WLProgress) is used by the app and imported by `worker/evidence.js`. Change targets logic there only.

- Worker: `worker/app.js` handles /api/app/* (password accounts on the Academy `learners` table, lockout after 8 failed logins, logbook sync, sign-off, verify, password reset by ZeptoMail, account deletion). The login cookie is the Academy cookie (`cookieFor` in academy.js), so one login covers both. Academy registration refuses to log in an email that has an app password.
- Privacy design: logbook text syncs to D1 `logbook_entries` (JSON per entry, 20 KB cap). Images never leave the device: IndexedDB per account (`wavelength-<id>`); the crop and cover editor re-encodes JPEG, which strips EXIF. Backups are a stored zip the learner shares to their own cloud. Never add server image storage without a DPIA.
- Sign-off: in person (finger signature on the learner's phone) or by link (14 days, token stored hashed, supervisor also signs by finger). Both record the basis (witnessed, reviewed, both). The server owns `signoff` on an entry and ignores client copies. It stores a hash of the signed fields, and editing any of them voids the sign-off (`signoffVoid`). Codes SO-XXXX-XXXX verify publicly, showing no findings.
- Sign-off status (October 2026): each scan shows a traffic light from `signState` in app.js. Green signed off, amber awaiting (a live link request; the app caches pending requests from /api/app/signoffs in kv `pending` on every sync), red needs action (not sent, queried, edited after sign-off, or link expired), no colour when the learner ticks "No sign-off needed" (`noSignoff` on the entry, unticked by default, not a signed field, so toggling never voids a sign-off). Logbook filter: Not sent, Awaiting, Signed off, No sign-off.
- Sign-off notifications: when sync brings in a new link or dashboard sign-off, the app shows a toast, a "New sign-off" card on Home, a dot on the Logbook tab and "New" chips (kv `news`, cleared on opening the logbook). The Worker also emails the learner on signoff/complete and dashboard sign-off (counts and code only) unless `profile.notifyEmail` is false (toggle under Me). In-person sign-offs do not notify. - Encrypted image sharing in sign-off links (built October 2026, switched on 4 October 2026 after Firas signed the DPIA "Wavelength DPIA: encrypted image and clip sharing for supervisor sign-off" in Claude Docs; review due October 2027). Switch: Worker var `IMAGE_SHARE` in `wrangler.jsonc` ("true" turns it on; `/api/app/me` returns `imageShare` and the app shows the option). Privacy notice, app terms and app page describe it; change them together with the feature. Ciphertext is deleted on sign-off, cancel and account deletion. Never store keys or plain images on the server.

## Supervisor lifecycle (built October 2026, not switched on)
- Fero's decisions (6 October 2026): approval lasts at most 12 months with admin review at every renewal (one-step "Confirm renewal" when nothing changed), no auto-renewal; a 30-day "Renewal under review" grace only when the renewal was sent before expiry by a fully current supervisor with nothing outstanding (`graceFor` in `worker/supervisor-lifecycle.js`); more-information requests never extend privileges. Registration exemption (admin, reason recorded) is never a verification exemption.
- Migrations `0012_supervisor_lifecycle.sql` and `0013_organisations.sql` were applied to production on 6 October 2026 (Fero's Step 1; Time Travel recovery point 2026-10-06T22:28:49Z, before the first write). `lifecycleReady()` still tolerates their absence. Every switch below, the daily cron and every later production step needs Fero's separate approval.
- Switches (Worker vars, all "false"): `SUPERVISOR_APPLY` (the application wizard; applicants use the registration functions through it, `PROFESSIONAL_VERIFY` stays off), `SUPERVISOR_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` (only approved supervisors can be linked and sign as approved; others show "Not yet verified by Wavelength").
- Approval is derived on every read, never stored as a flag: email verified, registration verified or exempt, required credentials current, `review_on` not passed (or within grace). Standing: none, applying, active, renewal_review, paused, expired, withdrawn, revoked.
- Four endings stay distinct: voluntary withdrawal, natural expiry, administrative revocation, rejection on verification or safety grounds. Revocation blocks one-off signing (`supervisor_blocks`) unless the admin records that one-off signing is permitted. Pending requests return to the learner, who reassigns, sends to a one-off assessor or cancels.
- Daily job `dailyLifecycle` (cron `15 5 * * *` in `wrangler.jsonc` triggers on PR #20, live only once that is merged and deployed with Fero's approval; routing in `worker/cron.js`: each cron runs exactly one job, an unknown cron runs nothing; `test/cron.test.mjs`): reminders at 60, 30 and 7 days, expiry, grace end, pauses, outbox retries, evidence purge (30 days after decision), stale drafts (90 days), rechecks, retention. Separate from the Tuesday newsletter cron. Each step is logged in `job_runs`. Never export anything but `default` from worker/index.js (`test/entry.test.mjs`).
- Email outbox `notification_log`: one row per key prevents routine duplicates, but a send whose result is lost is marked `unknown` and is not retried automatically, so "exactly once" is never claimed. The admin System tab resends or marks sent.
- Audit: `audit_events` (field names, counts and codes only, never personal data). Admin console: `/volunteer/admin/supervisors/` tabs New, More info, Renewals, Attention, Claims, Ended, People, System.
- Records: sign-offs and assessments keep `supervisor_snapshot` and a signer label (approved, account not approved, self-declared). Assessments can be amended by their dashboard assessor within 30 days as a new version (`amends`, `superseded_by`); the old one stays, marked superseded. Deleted accounts leave `code_tombstones` so verify says "withdrawn by its owner".
- Media fingerprints: the app stores a SHA-256 per image and clip in `entry.media`; sign-off hash version 2 covers them, so changing an image voids a newer sign-off. Typed signatures are recorded as `signatureKind: 'typed'` (assessment `data`, sign-off `ratings` key `$signatureKind`, entry `signoff.signatureKind`).
- App: encrypted uploads for sign-off links go through the IndexedDB `shares` queue (DB version 2, ladder in `openDb`): three retries, resume on launch, "Send without the failed ones". Images save at 2048 px quality 0.9 within the 1.5 MB share limit; clips at 1280 px, 4 Mbit/s. A dirty scan form asks before any navigation. Store builds send `X-Wavelength-App`; the update banner reads `/api/app/me` and a 426 `upgrade` answer, and a required update pauses sync only.
- Organisations: `data/organisations.json` seeds migration `0013_organisations.sql` (applied to production 6 October 2026, so never edit its seed again: add new organisations with `--out` to a new migration; `node tools/organisations-seed.mjs --check`). Ids are `wl-<n>`, never ODS codes. `GET /api/app/organisations?q=` feeds the app's combobox, which sends `organisationId` beside the text. Admin page `/volunteer/admin/organisations/`.
- Supervisor search (`SUPERVISOR_SEARCH`, needs 0012 and 0013): only current approvals whose holder opted in (`POST /api/app/supervisor/searchable`), up to 8 results, opaque refs, never an email. Add a search paragraph to the privacy notice before switching it on.
- Hardening: sign-up answers 202 `checkEmail` for any existing email (no 409), login failures are all the same 401 (no `noPassword`); `POST /api/app/logout-all`; every /api/app response carries `X-Request-Id`; auth routes are rate limited. Cloudflare Access on the admin area activates when `ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN` and `ACCESS_AUD` are set. Tools that write test data call `tools/local-only.mjs` and refuse anything but localhost.
- Tests: `test/supervisor-lifecycle.test.mjs`, `test/cron.test.mjs`, `test/signature-kind.test.mjs`, `test/entry.test.mjs`, `test/organisations.test.mjs`, `test/search.test.mjs`, `test/hardening.test.mjs`. Browser check: `node tools/e2e-supervisor-ui.mjs <outdir>` (local setup in its header).

## App Store and Google Play
- `mobile/` holds the Capacitor 8 apps (iOS and Android, bundle ID `uk.co.thewavelength.app`). The logbook ships inside the app: `node mobile/scripts/build-www.mjs` runs `node build.js` and assembles `mobile/www` (generated, not committed) from `dist/app`, with the Capacitor runtime, `native-config.js` (server address) and a Content Security Policy that allows API calls to thewavelength.co.uk only. Nothing in the app loads the website, which keeps Apple 4.2 and Google Play's webview policy satisfied. Listing text, privacy labels, Data safety answers and review notes: `mobile/STORE.md`.
- Web changes no longer reach store users on deploy: a change to `src/app/` ships in the next store build. Keep `/api/app/*` backwards compatible (older app versions stay installed), and never remove a field or route the app uses without a new store build first.
- `src/app/native.js` (window.WLNative) is the phone layer, inert on the website: server address, login token, website links in the in-app browser (`@capacitor/browser`), Android back button (`wl:back`), resume (`wl:resume`), haptics, app lock (`@capgo/capacitor-native-biometric`, Me > Security, asks at launch and after 90 s away). Where the website sets the cookie, the app receives `X-Wavelength-Token` (empty means logged out). Tests: `test/native.test.mjs`.
- App sign-ups that tick the newsletter are subscribed by the server (Zoho double opt-in), as the app cannot post the website's form.

The Android workflow runs `npm test`, builds the bundle, and outputs a signed .aab and .apk; it uploads to Play internal testing once `PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` exists.
