# Wavelength 1.0.12: test report

All results below are from the final source tree, after the review fixes, run on 9 October 2026 in the cloud
workspace. Everything ran locally:

- The Worker ran with node:sqlite standing in for D1, and every file in `migrations/` was applied.
- The app ran in Chromium (Playwright) over the stand-in native bridge.
- Data was synthetic: dummy accounts, generated test patterns and a generated "ultrasound-like" picture and clip.

No production system was contacted. **None of this replaces the physical-device checklists**
(`checklist-android.md`, `checklist-iphone.md`), which are all NOT RUN.

## Timing-sensitive checks (N11, N12)

- **What happened:** N11 (a flapping VPN must not cause more than 2 sync attempts in about 9 s) failed once in the
  final run, with 3 attempts. In four further reruns on 1.0.12, N11 failed once and N12 once. On the untouched 1.0.11
  tree all four reruns passed.
- **How it was checked:** to see whether 1.0.12 behaves differently, a probe logged every request in the same scenario,
  3 times per bundle.
- **What the probe showed:** both bundles made exactly 2 sync attempts during the loop. Recovery after unblocking took
  0.4 to 1.3 s on both. Nothing in the 1.0.12 diff touches sync scheduling.
- **Cause:** the third attempt appears when the jittered back-off lands inside the check's fixed window. The check has
  no margin for that, so it is a timing artefact of the check, not a regression.
- **Owner check:** it is covered on a phone by `checklist-android.md` 2.5 and 2.6.

## Performance

- **M7** compared scrolling Home and a long Progress page with the CPU slowed four times, with and without the reveal
  animation. With motion, the median frame was 16.7 ms and the 95th percentile 16.8 ms, with no frames over 50 ms.
  This matched reduced motion.
- **Editor:** while dragging, repaints are now limited to one a frame.
- **Clip preparation** runs in real time: a 10 s clip takes about 10 s.
