# Logo motion and sound: rules for the films, and recommendations for the app and website

Fero's decisions (10 October 2026):
- the cinematic logo reveal with sound belongs in the promotional films only;
- the app and website stay silent;
- reduced-motion preferences are respected;
- nobody waits through an opening animation.

This session changed only the films. The items in sections 2 and 3 are **recommendations** for the app and website
development sessions; nothing in the app, store builds or website was modified or deployed.

## 1. Promotional films (done, version 3)

- **Reveal:** about 3 s in the flagship, after the clinical problem; a 1.6 s variation opens the end frame of all three
  films. Component: `video/src/v3/reveal.jsx`.
- **Motion:**
  - a macro view of the wave on the W's right-hand upstroke, in soft teal light;
  - a smooth camera pullback with restrained tilt and a slight rotation (no spin, shake, flares, particles or flashes);
  - the emblem settles; then the wordmark and "Tune In · Gain Clarity".
- **Artwork check:** the settled logo is the approved artwork. `node video/scripts/logo-check.mjs` renders the final frame
  twice, once with the film's mark and once with the website's own SVG markup from `build.js`, and compares them:
  **0 of 36,100 pixels differ**.
- **Sound** (approved, `video/scripts/sfx-v3.mjs`): a soft air movement under the pullback and a quiet, warm bell tone
  as the logo settles.
  - Fundamental 392 Hz with soft upper partials and a low 98 Hz warmth.
  - No sharp high frequencies, no impact, short room only.
  - Mixed under the music and narration (the narration starts as the logo settles).
  - The films are mastered to -15 LUFS.
- **Viewer control:** the films are ordinary video files, so the normal player controls mute and unmute them.

### Version 4 update (10 October 2026)
Fero found the version 3 bell too long. Version 4 (`video/scripts/sfx-v4.mjs`, details in `18-videos-v4.md`):
- **Sound:** a shorter, warmer, soft-edged tone with a natural decay of about 0.7 s. Two notes, D5 rising to A5, the
  second exactly on the settle, with a single-note alternative for comparison.
- **Colours:** on light backgrounds the reveal uses the website's light-background logo colours (ring navy, wave
  #2A7F8A), checked pixel-identical.
- **Unchanged:** the app and website recommendations below still apply. Sound stays in the films only.

## 2. Website (recommendation for the website session)

| Topic | Today | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Header logo | Static mark; the optional `.draw` stroke animation is silent, and is switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`src/assets/style.css`) | Keep it silent. If a hero animation is added, keep it under about one second, silent, and off under reduced motion |
| Embedding the films | No `<video>` on the site yet | `<video controls playsinline preload="metadata" poster="...">` with `<track kind="captions" srclang="en" src="...vtt" default>`. If a film autoplays (for example a short hero loop), it must be `muted autoplay loop playsinline`, never autoplay with sound, and must not autoplay at all when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set (show the poster and a play button instead). Sound only after the visitor presses play or unmutes |
| Page speed | — | Lazy-load the film below the fold (`preload="none"` with a poster); host the 1080p file, not the 4K file |
| Privacy | — | Self-host the MP4 and VTT on thewavelength.co.uk. A third-party player (YouTube embed) would set cookies and needs consent under the privacy notice |

## 3. Android and iPhone apps (recommendation for the app session)

| Topic | Today | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Splash screen | Static brand splash generated by `capacitor-assets` (background #0A1526), shown only while the app loads; no sound | Keep it **silent and static** (or at most a fade under about 300 ms). No logo sound, no sound toggle, no cinematic reveal at start-up |
| Start-up time | The logbook opens offline from cache | Do not add any delay for animation; the splash should disappear as soon as the app is ready |
| Reduced motion | The app's motion (fades about 180 ms, scroll reveal) is already off under `prefers-reduced-motion` | Keep that rule for anything new |
| Where the reveal may appear | — | Optionally in "About Wavelength" or on the website, as a silent, skippable element; never blocking access to the logbook |

## 4. Accessibility summary

- Films: clean versions plus captioned versions; SRT and WebVTT files; no essential information in sound alone (the
  reveal sound is decorative).
- Reduced motion: respected on the website today. Any new site animation must follow the existing rule in
  `style.css`; the app already turns motion off.
- No forced waiting: the films are watched by choice; the app and website never play the reveal before showing
  content.
